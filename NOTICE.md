## What is not in this release

This repository is the open part of a larger internal knowledge repository. The
following are deliberately withheld and are not licensed under CC BY 4.0:

- the Sahai Fraud Verdict Engine and its source code
- the verdict gate: its rules, their ordering, and its response tables
- the regular expressions backing the `sig.detector.*` signals
- the regression and adversarial test corpora
- release signing key material

The withheld material is decision logic. Publishing the vocabulary tells a bad
actor nothing they cannot already read from the FTC, AARP, or IC3. Publishing
the decision logic would let them tune evasion against the exact conditions that
protect an older adult. That is the line this release draws.
